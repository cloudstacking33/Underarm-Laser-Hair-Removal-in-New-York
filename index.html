Frequently Asked Questions

How does underarm laser hair removal work?

The laser sends light into the hair follicle, where pigment absorbs it and weakens the follicle. Over several sessions, fewer hairs grow back and the ones that return are finer. Most clients plan a series of visits spaced a few weeks apart.

Is laser hair removal painful?

Most clients describe a mild, brief warmth or a light snap, and many find it easier than waxing. We adjust settings and technique for sensitive areas, and you can ask us to pause at any time.

How many sessions will I need?

The number depends on the area, your hair type and your skin tone. Many people need several sessions, and we explain a realistic plan during your consultation.

Is laser hair removal really permanent?

Laser can lead to a long lasting reduction in hair growth, and some people choose occasional maintenance visits. Hormones and genetics can influence results, so we always set honest expectations.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Shave the treatment area before you arrive, avoid sun exposure and tanning, and skip waxing or plucking in the weeks before. Come with clean skin and no lotions on the area.

Do you treat men as well as women?

Yes. We treat men and women, including the back, chest, neck, arms, legs and face, and we offer beard shaping laser hair removal for a clean, defined line.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash and credit cards.

What are your opening hours?
